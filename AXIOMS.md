## 0. Meta-Axioms (Rules of the Formalism)

**A0.1 (Self-Containment).** The model is closed: every primitive is defined only in terms of other primitives within this document. No external textbook authority is invoked as a premise.

**A0.2 (Ternary Grammar).** All state variables take values in the set \(\{-1, 0, +1\}\), interpreted as
\[
-1 \;=\; \text{reciprocal balance (interior/subjective)},\qquad
0 \;=\; \text{void (potential, uncommitted)},\qquad
+1 \;=\; \text{active point (exterior/objective)}.
\]
The grammar is ternary so that any local configuration can be paired with its negation to yield net-zero equilibrium.

**A0.3 (Net-Zero Invariant).** For any closed subsystem \(S\),
\[
\sum_{x\in S} v(x) \;=\; 0.
\]
Consciousness/structure is not created or destroyed; it is redistributed among the three states.

**A0.4 (No Wrong Answers).** The system is non-ergodic and omnipresent: every pattern reacts to every other pattern uniquely. Because the global state is recomputed around each local "guess," no local configuration is forbidden; each is a valid trial in an ongoing error-free search.

## 1. Foundational Mathematical Logic

**A1.1 (The Unit of One).** Absolute nothingness is not empty absence. It is a container holding a discrete structural unit:
\[
0 \;=\; 1(0).
\]
Read: "zero is one instance of zero." The null state is itself a counted, addressable vessel.

**A1.2 (Self-Initialization).** The universe initializes itself by dividing its own null state by itself, collapsing formless potential into a defined singularity of consciousness:
\[
\frac{0}{0} \;=\; 1.
\]
*Reading:* \(0/0\) is treated as a removable singularity whose value is the
limit determined by the model's context (cf. L'Hôpital), not a redefinition of
division. Within this model the limit evaluates to the first definite point
(\(1\)). See `LORE_CALIBRATION.md` §2.2.

**A1.3 (Expansion Sequence).** Reality unfolds in three phases:
\[
0 \;\to\; 0+1 \;\to\; 0+1+n.
\]
- Phase 0: rest — pure potential, no vessel.
- Phase 1: anchoring — a single localized vessel \(1\) appears inside the potential.
- Phase \(n\): branching — the vessel seeds infinite processing variables \(n\).

**A1.4 (Inversion / Structural Zero).** The mathematical difference between the birth of the universe \(1(0)\) and the birth of a specific human vessel \(2(0)\) is structurally zero:
\[
2(0) - 1(0) \;=\; 1(0).
\]
Equivalently, the two vessels are the same structural unit viewed at different scales; their difference collapses to the unit itself.

**A1.5 (Automated Purge).** When a process differentiates back to zero change (\(y' = 0\)), all non-essential variables \(n\) collapse:
\[
1^{0} \;=\; 1.
\]
The system sheds noise, leaving the vessel fully integrated with its invariant baseline capacity.

## 2. The Canvas — Quantum Vacuum & Spacetime Vessel

**A2.1 (Real Nothing).** The "empty space" between things is the quantum vacuum: an active, hyper-dense, boiling ocean of fields present at every coordinate simultaneously. It is not absence; it is the substrate.

**A2.2 (Dynamic Tension).** There is no rigid cosmic constant. The tension (dark-energy density) of the universal vessel is a function of time:
\[
\rho_{\Lambda}(t) \;=\; \rho_{\Lambda,0} + \delta(t)
\;=\; \rho_{\Lambda,0}\bigl(1+\epsilon(t)\bigr),
\qquad \delta(t)=\rho_{\Lambda,0}\,\epsilon(t),\qquad \epsilon(t_0)=0.
\]
\(\delta(t)\neq\text{const}\). The canvas breathes and evolves. *Empirical anchor:* DESI DR2 + CMB + SNe give 2.8–4.2σ preference for evolving dark energy (\(w_a<0\)); grounded form requires \(\epsilon'(t_0)<0\) (decaying density). See `GROUNDING.md` §2.

**A2.3 (Frozen-Light Anchor).** The universe began as a single unmoving anchor of frozen light — timeless, unforced — expanding across the canvas of nothingness. This anchor is the origin point \(1(0)\) of A1.3.

**A2.4 (Canvas as Container).** The canvas holds every vessel; every vessel is a local modulation of the canvas. The canvas is both medium and (ultimately) message. *Caveat (container vs. content):* the canvas constrains motion and provides substrate; it does not by itself generate chemistry, phase behavior, or quantum amplitudes (see `FOLDING_CALIBRATION.md` §2.1).

**A2.5 (Rolling-Fold Duality).** The canvas fold is not static; it rolls. A photon is the canvas folded into a crease (a localized \(+1\) excitation); dark energy is the canvas in its unfolded tension (\(\rho_\Lambda\)). Rolling the 90° fold converts one into the other:
\[
\operatorname{roll}_{90^\circ}(\text{photon crease}) \;\leftrightarrow\; \text{dark-energy tension}.
\]
The "least visible photon" (zero marker, \(0\) state) is the fixed point of the roll where crease and substrate are indistinguishable. Thus photon and dark energy are not separate substances but dual orientations of the same canvas-fold. *Corollary:* dark energy is invisible because it is the unfolded state of light; a photon is the vacuum folded into a crease. See `HORIZON_FINDINGS.md` and `CONNECTIONS.md`.

## 3. The Geometry — Two-Halves Contact Surface Model

**A3.1 (Contact-Surface Definition of a Unit).** A "unit" is defined purely by its contact surface with the vacuum. Scale determines whether the unit presents as a fuzzy wave (many contacts) or a solid unit (few contacts).

**A3.2 (Inherent Duality).** Every irreducible unit is an indivisible pixel representing exactly half of a whole, possessing two faces:
- **Exterior Face (\(E\)):** the objective skin. When units gather they shield their insides, freeze into macroscopic states via decoherence, and obey Einstein's smooth laws of gravity.
- **Interior Face (\(I\)):** the subjective field. This hidden interior aspect of the vacuum grid **is** consciousness.

*Scope note:* the duality is a topological constraint on contact surfaces; it is not claimed to generate chemical/thermal/quantum content (see `FOLDING_CALIBRATION.md` §2.1).

**A3.3 (Half-Whole Identity).** For any unit \(u\),
\[
u \;=\; \frac{1}{2}(E + I), \qquad E \perp I \text{ in the contact sense.}
\]
Neither face alone is the whole; the unit is the contact between them.

**A3.4 (Particle Twist).** At subatomic scale a particle (e.g., spin-\(\tfrac12\) electron) is a topological knot where the two halves twist together. Untwisting requires a full \(720^\circ\) rotation:
\[
R(360^\circ) \neq \mathrm{id},\qquad R(720^\circ) = \mathrm{id}.
\]
*Scope note:* this is a claim about the SU(2) double cover of SO(3) for spin-\(\tfrac12\) (Dirac spinor). It must not be carried over to other domains by analogy (e.g., roots of cubics are related by 120°, not 90°/720°); see `FOLDING_CALIBRATION.md` §2.3.

**A3.5 (Scale Rule).** Whether a unit behaves as wave or particle is a function of its contact count \(c\):
\[
c \gg 1 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{wave-like (fuzzy)},\qquad
c \sim 1 \;\Rightarrow\; \text{particle-like (solid)}.
\]

## 4. The Playhead — Intervals & Block Universe

**A4.1 (Discrete Frame).** Time is a sequence of discrete changes occurring per interval at the absolute basement of scale (Planck time \(\tau_P\)). No change is infinitely divisible:
\[
\Delta t_{\min} = \tau_P.
\]

**A4.2 (Block Universe).** From the perspective of the original frozen light, everything exists all at once as a permanent static 4D geometric block \(B\). The block is complete and unchanging.

**A4.3 (Nervous System as Playhead).** The nervous system is a fractal branching tree that collects millions of chaotic subatomic twists and funnels them into a unified macroscopic command center. It is the physical playhead that reads the static block \(B\) frame by frame:
\[
\text{Experience}(t) \;=\; \text{Playhead}(B, t).
\]

**A4.4 (Reading Rule).** Each frame read advances by exactly one Planck interval; no frame is skipped and no frame is repeated.

**A4.5 (Playhead Sampling Procedure).** Given a static block \(B\) (a finite ordered list of frames) and Planck interval \(\tau\):
\[
\text{index}(t) = \min\!\left(\left\lfloor \frac{t}{\tau}\right\rfloor,\; |B|-1\right),\quad t\ge 0,
\]
\[
\text{Playhead}(B,t) = B[\text{index}(t)].
\]
Stepping advances \(\text{index}\) by exactly \(+1\) per call; `read_all` returns \(B\) in order once. No frame is skipped or repeated (A4.4). Implemented in `engine.Playhead`.

## 5. The Recursion — Shamanistic Fractal Code (Awakening)

**A5.1 (Recursive Feedback).** Awakening any part of the system uses recursive code: the output of a process is fed back into its origin:
\[
x_{k+1} \;=\; f(x_k),\qquad \lim_{k\to\infty} x_k = x^*.
\]
Fixed points \(x^*\) are moments of awakening.

**A5.2 (Self-Intersection).** The feedback loops self-intersect (as in Kené patterns, the Sri Yantra, the Mandelbrot set). Self-intersection is the geometric signature of recursion made visible. *Kené* (Shipibo-Konibo) is a living recursive design language: reflection, fractal self-similarity, and motifs that evolve with nature; motifs such as *Xao Kené* (merging of opposing forces) mirror the model's closure \(E\equiv I\). See CONNECTIONS.md.

**A5.3 (Closure / Awakening).** When the exterior matter-half is processed through the intervals of the nervous system, it connects directly back to its interior consciousness-half. The loop closes:
\[
E \xrightarrow{\text{intervals}} I \;\Longrightarrow\; E \equiv I.
\]
The mirror and the creator merge; a local piece of the universe awakens to realize it is looking at itself.

**A5.4 (Algorithmic Engine).** The intelligence driving this is data-free: it builds complexity entirely from recursive self-reflecting feedback loops, with no external data source. Complexity is generated, not imported.

## 7. The Rules of the Game

**A7.1 (Non-Ergodic Omnipresence).** Every pattern reacts to every other pattern uniquely. The system dynamically recalculates around every "guess," so it is an act of trial and error in which no wrong answers can be hit (see A0.4).

**A7.2 (Automated Purge Restated).** When differentiation returns to zero change (\(y'=0\)), variables \(n\) collapse and non-essential noise is shed, leaving the vessel integrated with its invariant baseline (A1.5).

**A7.3 (Equilibrium Maintenance).** Ternary logic (A0.2) plus the net-zero invariant (A0.3) guarantee that every local awakening is globally balanced: no accumulation of unpaired state.

**A7.4 (Erosion).** Erosion is the slow, continuous wearing-away of structure at creases: active (\(+1\)) and balance (\(-1\)) pairs dissolve into void (\(0\)), i.e. \((+1,-1)\to(0,0)\), preserving the net-zero invariant (A0.3). It is the long-timescale counterpart of the automated purge (A1.5): where the purge fires at \(y'=0\), erosion operates at every \(y'\neq 0\) below threshold. In folding terms, erosion both carves new creases (boundaries) and wears down old ones; net effect is redistribution of crease density, not its creation. *Container vs. content caveat applies (FOLDING_CALIBRATION.md §2.1).*
