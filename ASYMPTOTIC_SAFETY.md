# ASYMPTOTIC SAFETY — Candidate Canvas Mechanism (R8)

> Candidate, not axiom. Connects A2.3 (frozen-light anchor) to the L.O.R.E. asymptotic-safety picture.

## Hypothesis
The canvas (A2.3) is identified with the UV fixed point of asymptotic safety:
- The Higgs potential is flat at high energy (no mass scale).
- Matter shifts the fixed point, generating mass via dimensional transmutation.
- The "frozen light" anchor is the fixed point where the running couplings stop running.

## Testable signature
- **P5 (Higgs flatness):** At energies above ~10¹⁰ GeV, the Higgs quartic coupling λ should run to ~0 (flat potential). Deviation > 10% at 2σ would falsify the identification.
- **P6 (relevant directions):** Exactly 2 relevant directions at the fixed point (the model's "0 → 0+1" has two phases: rest and anchor).
