## 1. What it is

A non-Euclidean ML framework mapping global human ontologies into a conformal
Poincaré disk to detect Out-of-Distribution (OOD) anomalies on the boundary
horizon (\(r \to 1\)). Human knowledge branches like a tree; hyperbolic space
expands exponentially near its borders, fitting hierarchical data perfectly.

Key formulations:
- **Poincaré distance:** \(d_H(u,v) = \text{arccosh}(1 + 2\frac{\|u-v\|^2}{(1-\|u\|^2)(1-\|v\|^2)})\)
- **Novelty score:** \(N(x_q) = \|x_q\|_2 \in [0,1)\) — proximity to boundary = novelty.
- **Antiderivative of universals:** \(\int \mathcal{M}_{\text{knowledge}}(\theta)\,d\theta = \mathcal{U}(\theta) + C_0\), with \(\lim_{r\to 0} F(\theta) = C_0\).
- **\(C_0\):** the foundational field of pure awareness / unified systemic potentiality at the center.
