# HORIZON FINDINGS — Academic Memo

> Status: Research memo, 2026-10-27. Topic: cosmic horizons, dark energy, and the
> accelerating-expansion controversy, with implications for the model.

---

## 1. Abstract

The model's grounded front (A2.2, `GROUNDING.md`) rests on the claim that dark
energy is dynamical (\(\delta(t)\neq 0\)) and that the universe is accelerating.
Both claims are now under active dispute. A 2026 re-analysis of >1,700
supernovae (Sarkar et al.) argues the apparent acceleration is directional and
may be an artifact of uncorrected host-galaxy age evolution and local bulk
flow; a concurrent analysis (Vincenzi et al.) defends acceleration. DESI DR2
still prefers evolving dark energy at 2.8–4.2σ but has not reached 5σ. Euclid
will provide a decisive test (FoM > 400). The model must therefore treat
\(\delta(t)\) as a *hypothesis under test*, not a settled fact, and must
distinguish **local** (near-present derivative) from **global** (integrated)
claims.

## 2. Horizon concepts

| Horizon | Definition | Relevance to model |
|---|---|---|
| Particle horizon | Farthest distance light has traveled since Big Bang | Playhead's read limit (A4.3): frames beyond are unreadable |
| Event horizon | Boundary beyond which events never reach observer | "Purge" boundary (A1.5): non-essential variables never reach the vessel |
| Hubble horizon | \(c/H(t)\); where recession speed = c | Canvas tension scale; where \(\rho_\Lambda\) competes with matter |
| Cosmic event horizon (accelerating universe) | Shrinking comoving boundary deleting distant galaxies | Long-timescale erosion (A7.4) at cosmic scale |

In an accelerating universe the event horizon shrinks: galaxies cross it and
"disappear" — a literal cosmic erosion of observable structure.

## 3. The 2026 acceleration controversy

**Challenge (Sarkar et al., 2026):**
- Re-analysis of >1,700 SNe Ia with stellar-age correction.
- Apparent acceleration becomes anisotropic, aligned with local bulk flow
  (CMB dipole direction), and fades with distance.
- If acceleration is directional, a uniform dark-energy cause is ruled out.

**Defense (Vincenzi et al., 2026):**
- Same-era analysis concludes observations still support ongoing acceleration
  and that host-age corrections do not erase the signal.

**Implication for the model:** the "breathing canvas" (\(\epsilon'(t_0)<0\))
is a *local* near-present claim and survives either way; the *global*
claim that density was higher in the past at \(z\sim1\) is sample- and
analysis-dependent (see `GROUNDING.md` §2.4). The model must not overstate.

## 4. DESI status

- DR2: 3.1σ (DESI+CMB), 2.8–4.2σ with SNe.
- Still below the 5σ discovery threshold.
- Preference for quintom B (\(w_0>-1,\;w_a<0\)) persists across SNe samples.

## 5. Euclid as decisive test

- Q1 (2025): 0.45% of survey.
- CLOE forecast: \(F_{\rm oM}(w_0,w_a) > 400\) (all primary probes).
- Expected to settle whether acceleration is real and whether \(\delta(t)\) is
  globally negative.

   (open question, not axiom).

   event horizon: structure beyond the horizon is eroded from observability.
4. **Hold the sync point (A6) and awakening (A5) claims** as explicitly
   non-empirical regardless of the acceleration debate outcome.
