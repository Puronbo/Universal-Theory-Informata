# GLOSSARY — Formal Definitions

- **Canvas:** quantum vacuum substrate; holds all vessels.
- **Contact surface:** defines a unit by its interface with the vacuum.
- **Crease:** non-integrable point where Nijenhuis tensor ≠ 0; locus of interaction.
- **Erosion:** slow wearing-away of (+1,-1) pairs into (0,0), preserving net-zero.
- **Exterior face (E):** objective skin; decoherence, gravity.
- **Interior face (I):** subjective field; consciousness.
- **Net-zero invariant:** sum of ternary states in a closed subsystem = 0.
- **Playhead:** reads the static 4D block frame by frame at Planck intervals.
- **Purge:** collapse of non-essential variables when y'=0.
- **Rolling-fold duality:** photon (crease) ↔ dark energy (unfolded tension).
- **Stroboscopic sampling:** discrete sampling of a continuous process → frozen alias.
- **Ternary grammar:** states {-1, 0, +1}.
- **Void (0):** potential, uncommitted; also "Real Nothing" substrate.
