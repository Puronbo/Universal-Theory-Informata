## The unifying principle

Every phenomenon is a **different operation on the same canvas**, distinguished by:

1. **Which face** — Exterior (objective) vs. Interior (subjective).
2. **Which scale** — Planck pixel vs. biological equator vs. cosmic horizon.
3. **Which operation** — fold/crease vs. unfold/tension vs. stretch vs. sample vs. erode.

---

## The key differences

### Light vs. sound
- Light = excitation **of the canvas itself** (vacuum) → no medium needed, travels at \(c\).
- Sound = excitation **of matter already on the canvas** → needs a medium, far slower.
- Folding analogy: light is a **fold** (crease on the sheet); sound is a **vibration of the folded paper**.

### Light vs. dark energy
- Same entity **rolled 90°** (A2.5). Photon = folded crease; dark energy = unfolded tension.
- Never seen simultaneously — dual orientations, like two sides of a coin.

### Gravity vs. light
- Gravity is not a wave on the canvas; it **is** the canvas stretching (A3.6).
- Light propagates *through* stretched space; gravity *is* the stretching.

### Consciousness vs. everything else
- Everything above is Exterior (objective). Consciousness is the Interior (subjective) face.
- Not a wave or particle — the medium's capacity to **fold back on itself** (A5.3).
- Operates at the stroboscopic sample rate of the nervous system, not at \(c\).
